# Name candidates, 2026-10-09

Status: **brainstorm and first-pass check, for the owner to read.** Written while the owner was out, at their request for as many possibilities as the language offers, each one researched. Background and the reason for a rename are in `docs/name-knockout-2026-10-08.md`.

What every name here had to meet:

- Not tied to one condition, illness or neurodivergence.
- Makes sense on first hearing, even when it is two words pushed together.
- Short and easy to say.
- Sits beside the open book icon (book, page, leaf, story), or at least does not fight it.

**What "checked" means here.** Each name was searched on the web for apps, companies and brands using it. That is a knockout pass only. It does not search the USPTO, the UK, EU or Canada registers, Google Play in full, domains or handles, and nothing here is legal advice. A name marked clean has only cleared the first gate. Nothing was bought, filed or sent to anybody.

Verdicts:

- **Clean**: no app, company or brand found using it.
- **Caution**: something close exists, in a different trade or under a slightly different spelling. Usable, but the full search has to look hard at it.
- **Out**: an app or company in a nearby field already uses it, or it is too easily misheard.

My pick for the full search, if you want three: **Storyroot, Turnleaf, Lifestead.** The first keeps the most of what you already have; the second is the best match for an open book that is also about growing things; the third is the widest "everything about a person's life" name.
