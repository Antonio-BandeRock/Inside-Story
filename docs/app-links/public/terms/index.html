1. What Inside Story is

Inside Story is an app for keeping the records of your life in one place: food and meals, health, medicines, symptoms, routines, garden, home, money and more. It offers general information alongside those records and points out patterns in what you have logged. By using the app you agree to these terms and to the Privacy Policy.

2. It is not medical advice

  • What the app shows is general information and your records. It is not medical advice, and it does not diagnose, treat or prevent any condition.
  • It does not replace your doctor, pharmacist, dietitian or any other health professional. Check with your doctor before making any medical decision.
  • Never start, stop, skip or change a medicine or its dose because of something in the app. That decision belongs to you and your prescriber.
  • A pattern the app points out comes from one person's records and can be chance. It is something to look into and talk over, never a finding about your body.
  • Research summaries in the app carry a note of how strong the evidence is. Some describe early or limited evidence, and say so.

3. Allergies

The app is allergen-aware and never allergy-safe. It flags ingredients you have told it about, using food data from public databases, recipes and product labels, any of which can be wrong, incomplete or out of date. It can miss an allergen. If you have an allergy, read every label and ask when eating out.

4. Emergencies

Nobody watches what is entered in the app, and it cannot call anyone for you. In an emergency, call your local emergency number.

5. Your records and your backups

  • Your records are yours. They are kept on your device and, if you choose, encrypted in a cloud folder in your account.
  • You are responsible for keeping a backup. Without one, a lost or broken device means lost records.
  • The password that protects your backup is known only to you. It cannot be recovered or reset by anyone, and a lost password means the backup cannot be opened.

Locked means locked. Even we can't get in. Especially we can't get in.

6. Sharing with other people

You can connect with other people who use the app and share parts of your records with them. Share only what you are comfortable with them seeing, and only records about other people that you have their permission to keep and share. Once shared, what the other person does with it is between the two of you.

7. Information from other sources

Food, product, medicine label, weather and growing zone information comes from public sources such as Open Food Facts, USDA FoodData Central, openFDA and Open-Meteo. It is shown as those sources provide it and may contain errors. Links to outside websites and research are for reading; we do not control those sites.

8. Gardening, food preparation and other practical guidance

Guidance on growing, preserving, fermenting and preparing food follows published sources. Food safety depends on how it is carried out. Follow tested methods, and when a step is about safety (canning, fermenting, foraging), check it against a trusted local source.

  • Do not copy the app or its written content to sell or publish as your own.
