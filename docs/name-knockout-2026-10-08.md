# The name "Inside Story": trademark knockout search, 2026-10-08

Status: **open decision.** Recorded 2026-10-08. A preliminary knockout search on the name was run the same day (web research only, not legal advice). This file holds what it found, what it got wrong about the app, and the options. Nothing was bought, filed or sent to anybody.

The original naming record (the candidates ruled out in July, the first USPTO check, the domain checks) is in `docs/CLAUDE-ARCHIVE.md` under "App name: Inside Story". Understory and Body of Work cleared that first check and lost on fit, so they are the obvious first names to search again if the app is renamed.

## Correction to how the search described the app

The search described "a mobile app for people living with Hashimoto's thyroiditis to track symptoms, medication, and health over time, optionally shared with a partner or carer, made by Ghostead, with data kept on the user's devices." The partner or carer sharing, Ghostead, and local-first storage are right. The rest is too narrow:

- The app covers **19 autoimmune conditions** individually and in combination, Hashimoto's among them and not ahead of them. **IBD, IBS and celiac are three of the 19**, and gut healing is a stated goal. That is the exact ground "Inside Story: A Gut Journal" stands on, so the overlap is closer than the search assumed.
- It is also a daily-living app for a second audience with no condition at all (ADHD, autism, anyone carrying too much), plus food, garden, money, family and a long-form reading library.
- Platforms: Android, iPhone, Windows and Mac.

Classes: 9 (downloadable software), 42 (software as a service) and 44 (health information) were the right ones. **Class 41** (education, the reading library and the planned Learn decks) should be added to any later search.

## Main finding: high risk

**"Inside Story: A Gut Journal"**, a live iOS app (Health & Fitness, developer Jayden Vann Hanks), released 2026-07-30, version 1.1 on 2026-08-08. It tracks symptoms, food, medication, mood and appointments for IBD, IBS and celiac, with a $9.99 a month Pro tier, the same price as our Individual tier. No US trademark filing by this developer was found, but use in commerce gives them common-law rights, and confusion in the stores is likely.
https://apps.apple.com/us/app/inside-story-a-gut-journal/id6787264326

On priority: our name was chosen around 2026-07-18 and `insidestoryapp.com` registered then, but Inside Story has not been offered in any store, and a placeholder site is weak evidence of use in commerce for software. In the US, rights in a mark come from use in commerce or from an intent-to-use filing, and we have neither. Their 2026-07-30 release is very likely the earlier use.

## Medium risks

- **insidestory.app**, a book and author platform in beta, run by a sole trader in Auckland, NZ (about 2,745 readers, 634 authors). Uses @insidestoryapp on Facebook and @insidestory.app on Instagram. No trademark found. Different field, but it splits search results and takes the handle we would want. https://insidestory.app , https://insidestory.app/terms
- **The phrase is crowded and weak**: Al Jazeera's daily "Inside Story" programme and podcast, an Australian magazine (insidestory.org.au), an Australian TV programme, and gut health books ("Gut: The Inside Story"). A crowded phrase is easy to register narrowly and hard to enforce, so even a clean registration would protect us little.

## Gaps in the search

Google Play was only partly searched, handles were not confirmed on each platform's signup, and the UK, EU and Canada registers were not searched directly. Since the app is international in scope, those registers matter for any name it ends up with.

## What a rename would touch

The cost of a rename rises sharply at the first store release, so it is cheapest now.

- **Free to change before release:** the store name, the Android package `com.insidestoryapp.app` (fixed for good once published on Google Play), the desktop installer, the icon's word mark, and roughly 225 places the name appears in app text.
- **Concepts built on the phrase:** "A life's Inside Story" (the stated end point of the app), The Inside Story section of Your Story, and the planned Inside Knowledge card. The idea under each survives a rename; the wording would need a new home.
- **Unaffected:** Ghostead, the company, and everything built.
- **Would need new ones:** domains, the App Link host (`insidestoryapp.com`), the Cloudflare Worker's custom domain, and the terms and privacy pages on the site.

## Options

1. **Rename before launch.** Brainstorm, then run the same knockout search on a shortlist: both app stores, USPTO classes 9, 41, 42 and 44, the UK, EU and Canada registers, .app and .com domains, and handles.
2. **Keep the name and add a distinguishing word.** A thyroid word would contradict the app's scope (no single condition leads it). Any other word still leaves "Inside Story" as the part people remember, which is the part a confusion test looks at.
3. **Keep the name as it is, after a trademark attorney reviews the Gut Journal conflict.**

## Recommendation (Claude, 2026-10-08)

**Option 1, rename before launch**, and spend the attorney's time clearing the new name rather than defending this one. Their use came first, their conditions are three of ours, their price matches ours, and the phrase is too crowded to defend even if we won. Option 2 keeps the same collision under a longer name. Option 3 pays an attorney to most likely confirm the problem.
