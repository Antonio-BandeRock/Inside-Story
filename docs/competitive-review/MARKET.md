# Inside Story: market and advertising case (2026-10-07)

Published page: https://claude.ai/artifact/KGZLwhE3abMRCsWBpQCKeM (the full version, with charts and sources). This file is the plain copy kept with the build plan.

Tiers: **measured** (study, government count, company report), **survey** (poll, may be skewed), **estimate** (worked out here, arithmetic shown).

## The answer in six lines

1. Lead with five audiences: autoimmune conditions, adults with ADHD or autism, family caregivers, people who grow food, people who want to leave their story behind.
2. Lead every message with privacy. 53% of Americans would never trust a tech company with health data; Inside Story holds none.
3. Paid installs do not pay back in year one at median conversion. Spend at scale only once 30-day retention is known.
4. Advertise contextually: patient and ADHD creators, podcast host reads, newsletters, keyword search ads. No pixels, no retargeting.
5. A launch quarter that could become a sensation: $150K to $300K. Sustained: $1M to $3M a year. Creator gifting ($0) and the ground floor (under $2K a month) should start now; every paid level is optional and waits on retention.
6. Most companion tiers are priced above leaders that are free. Caregiver is a feature inside the Free version, turned on and paid for by the person cared for, and it covers only the people cared for; the caregiver uses Free for their own records unless they pay for themselves (decided 2026-10-07, clarified 2026-10-08); the rest of P27 matters more than any ad budget.

## Arithmetic of paid installs (per 1,000 installs)

Install to trial about 6%; trial to paid median 39.9% (RevenueCat 2025, health and fitness); value before the paywall 1.5x to 2x.

| | Median | Value first |
|---|---|---|
| Paying | 24 | 40 |
| Year-one revenue at $76.49 (after 15% store fee) | $1,836 | $3,060 |
| Cost at $3 an install | $3,000 | $3,000 |
| Cost at $8 an install | $8,000 | $8,000 |

RevenueCat's measured median is $1.21 revenue per install at 12 months. Health trackers keep about 6% at 30 days. Measure with store consoles, per-creator codes and per-campaign landing paths, never in-app tracking.

## Kind of advertising

- Patient and neurodivergent micro-creators: $100 to $500 an Instagram post, $25 to $800 a TikTok video. The biggest channel.
- Podcast host reads: $25 to $40 CPM, health about $27. $30K is about a million listens.
- Apple Search Ads and Play keyword ads: $1.59 to $1.88 a tap; plan $3 to $8 an install.
- Newsletters and communities: condition foundations, caregiver organisations, gardening and permaculture lists, invited subreddits.
- Earned press: the founder story, the no-data health app, 19 conditions at once.
- App Store featuring: App of the Day lifted downloads about 685% the next week; pitched through Apple's free nomination form.

## Levels of spend

| Level | Spend | Buys |
|---|---|---|
| Creator gifting | $0 | free Individual access for 50 to 100 small creators in the condition, ADHD, autism, caregiving and gardening communities; no payment, no script, a code each, FTC disclosure; start now |
| Ground floor | $0 to $2K a month | store keywords, reading pages on insidestoryapp.com, beta from each community, waiting list |
| Seeding (optional) | $5K to $15K a month | 20 to 60 creator posts, $3K to $5K keyword ads, 1 to 2 podcast reads |
| Launch quarter (optional) | $150K to $300K | about $60K podcasts, $50K to $80K creators, $50K keyword ads, $20K to $40K film and press, App Store nomination |
| Sustained (optional) | $1M to $3M a year | always-on creators, podcasts and newsletters, companion tiers launched as events, caregiver partnerships |

Launch-year install estimate with a feature and creators that land: 250K to 1M. Finch: 2.34M downloads in 90 days, mostly word of mouth. Yuka: about 80M users, 25M in the US, no advertising ever.

## Risks

Logging drop-off (6% at 30 days for trackers); ten tabs reading as too much; iPhone release needed before the launch quarter (iOS converts about 2x and only Apple features); health claims in ads must pass the app's five never-say rules; companion pricing.
