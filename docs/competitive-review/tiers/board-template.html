What would someone pay Inside Story for?

Every function in the app, built and planned, plus the companion tiers, placed by two questions: is it already free in other apps (left) or only found here (right), and would someone pay for it on its own (bottom to top)? The tilted line splits Free from paid. Everything is a draft for you to drag.

How to use the board
  • Drag any dot to where you think it belongs. Your placement is saved and stays when the page is reopened.
  • Tap a dot to read why I put it there, why other apps give that kind of thing away or charge for it (with sources), what Inside Story does differently, and to pin it to Free or paid whatever the line says.
  • Hollow dots with a dashed edge are not built yet, are waiting on a decision, or are companion tiers.
  • Below the lists: the research behind every reason, and how more of the app could reach pay caliber.
  • Drag the two square handles on the line up or down. Everything above the line is paid, everything below is Free.
  • Red rings are safety items. They stay Free on every tier (P28), so they cannot be pinned to paid.
  • Bigger dots are used more often: daily, weekly, or now and then.
  • The "would pay" scores are my guesses until the beta (P2) gives us something better.
