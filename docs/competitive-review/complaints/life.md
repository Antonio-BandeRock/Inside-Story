# What users of the Life competitors complain about

Researched 2026-10-09. Same format as `food.md`: dislikes, wishes (fix, change, add) and useless, each with source, date (the date read where the source gives none) and recurrence, ending with what Lifestead must do so the same complaint cannot be made of it.

Recurrence key: **many** (raised across many reviews or posters, or measured as a share of reviews), **several** (two to five people), **one** (a single person, kept because it is specific and checkable).

The same caution as `food.md` applies: justuseapp, Kimola, Unstar, appfollow, marlvel and appshunter are review-analysis or SEO sites, some of them competitors. Their percentages are reported as they claim them, not verified. heynori, remento and terms.law write about the apps they compare against, so read them the same way.

Already covered elsewhere: AnyList (including AnyList household) in `food.md`; Google Fit in `trends.md`; Monarch Money in `insights.md`; Medisafe Medfriend and Tiimo family in `schedules.md`; Clue Connect in `signals.md`. "The phone's contacts", "Phone contacts", "The phone's dialer and messages", "Medical ID (iPhone) and Emergency information (Android)", "Countdown apps" and "Resume builders" are composites or parts of the phone; their named members (Google Contacts, Dreamdays, Zety, Resume.io, Kickresume) have sections below. "FEMA app and EWG Tap Water Database" is the two apps below. Everplans, Sheer Health, Giki, Joro, Examine and the Job Accommodation Network had no dated complaints found; Everplans and Examine are described below for what they cover, the rest are left for a later pass. LinkedIn's complaints are about a social network and a job market rather than a record somebody keeps, so it has no section.

---

## Across the whole category first

1. **Trials that become charges the person did not choose, and cancelling that does not stop them.** Zety's $2.70 trial at $23.70 a month, Resume.io and Kickresume billing after cancelling, Ancestry's $250M auto-renewal suit, MyHeritage's trial to an annual plan. **many**
2. **Features moved behind a payment after people relied on them free** (Cozi's calendar cut to about 30 days, TickTick's calendar and Siri, Huckleberry's sleep predictions). **many**
3. **A device or service that stopped working when the company dropped it** (Awair Home, Airthings' new app shutting out older monitors, OmniFocus' web version gone when a subscription ends). **many**
4. **Records lost moving to a new phone or restoring a backup** (WhatsApp from Google Drive, Signal before Secure Backups, Huckleberry after updates, StoryWorth stories not saved). **many**
5. **The household or family is not one record**: Huckleberry's feeds not syncing between parents, Things with no sharing at all, FamilySearch's open tree edited by strangers. **many**
6. **Alerts that miss or repeat** (Red Cross: none, or seven for one storm; Mayo Clinic: only phone calls arrive; CaringBridge: no notice of a comment). **several**

**What Lifestead must do across the board:** the across-the-board line in `home.md` on trials and cancelling holds here too; a function never moves from Free to paid once people use it; a device reading is a record kept on the phone, so a maker dropping its app leaves the history intact and typed readings still work (the growing-conditions rule, `garden_readings`, applied to air and water too); every move to a new phone is a full restore checked against a fingerprint before the old copy is let go; a household is one shared record through the tier model, and nobody outside the people the person invited can edit it; one alert per event, said once, with the setting that sent it named.

## Cozi (leader: family calendar and lists)

**Dislikes**
- In 2024 the free tier became a rolling calendar of roughly 30 days with ads; Gold is $39 a year. **many** ([heynori](https://heynori.com/blog/best-shared-family-calendar-apps/cozi-vs-google-calendar-for-families), read 2026-10-09)
- Ads feel out of place in a family tool. **many** ([marlvel](https://marlvel.ai/apps/com-cozi-icozi-free/vs), read 2026-10-09)

**Lifestead must:** no ads, and the whole calendar, past and future, is the person's on every tier.

## FamilyWall (family organiser)

**Dislikes**
- Updates removed or disabled widgets people used. **several** ([Kimola](https://kimola.com/reports/discover-key-insights-from-familywall-app-feedback-analysis-app-store-us-156051), read 2026-10-09)
- Crashes on iPhone. **several** ([justuseapp](https://justuseapp.com/en/app/496889629/familywall-family-organizer/problems), read 2026-10-09)

**Lifestead must:** a widget or Home card, once offered, is never taken away by an update without saying where it went.

## Baby Connect (baby tracker)

4.8 from 17k ratings, while justuseapp reads 73.6% of the reviews it analysed as negative.

**Dislikes**
- Charged for a subscription after already paying for the app. **several** ([justuseapp](https://justuseapp.com/en/app/326574411/baby-connect-baby-tracker/reviews), read 2026-10-09)
- Cloud storage protected by nothing more than a simple password. **one** (same)
- The interface needs work; nothing connects to other apps. **several** (same)

**Lifestead must:** a child's records sit under the Guardian tier, encrypted on the device and in the person's own cloud folder, never on a company server; anything bought once stays bought.

## Huckleberry (leader: baby sleep)

**Dislikes**
- SweetSpot sleep predictions are paid. **many** ([Unstar](https://unstar.app/blog/huckleberry-baby-tracker-sprout-glow-baby-daybook-baby-tracker-apps-ranked-2026), read 2026-10-09)
- Feeds logged by one parent do not reach the other. **several** (same)
- Data lost after updates; notifications misfire. **several** (same)
- Plus members shown pop-ups to upgrade to Premium. **several** (same)

**Lifestead must:** two parents write to one record (the merge between people, extended to the Guardian tier); a paying person is never shown an upgrade pop-up.

## CaringBridge (leader: health updates to family and friends)

4.8 from 23.9K ratings.

**Dislikes**
- Buggy and freezes. **several** ([justuseapp](https://justuseapp.com/en/app/365726944/caringbridge/reviews), read 2026-10-09)
- No notice when somebody comments. **several** (same)
- Slow or rude support; no confirmation that a donation went through. **several** ([marlvel](https://marlvel.ai/apps/caringbridge), read 2026-10-09)

**Liked:** a lifeline for keeping everyone told during a medical crisis without repeating it to each person. **many** (same)

**Lifestead must:** a caregiver update reaches the chosen people through the caregiver link without a company server, and a reply raises a notice the person can answer from.

## Everplans (leader: end-of-life and legacy planning)

No dated complaints found. Its My To-Dos cover My Life, Health & Medical, Financial, Legal, Eldercare and After I'm Gone ([myshortlister](https://myshortlister.com/everplans/vendor-reviews), read 2026-10-09). Left for a later pass.

## Trustworthy (family records)

## StoryWorth (leader: a life story as a printed book)

**Dislikes**
- The subscription ran out without notice, and books were not received. **several** ([thingtesting](https://thingtesting.com/brands/storyworth/reviews?p=2), read 2026-10-09)

## Ancestry (leader: family tree and records)

**Dislikes**

- Cancelling needed a phone call two days before the trial ended; auto-renewal has led its 1-star reviews for five years. **many** ([deceptive.design](https://deceptive.design/articles/ancestrycom-difficult-subscription-cancellation-and-misleading-free-trial), read 2026-10-09)
- Records sit behind further payments after joining. **many** (same)

**Lifestead must:** the family tree (item 34) is the person's, in and out as GEDCOM, never with a record behind a second payment.

## MyHeritage (family tree and DNA)

**Dislikes**
- Free trials convert to annual plans, and refunds are hard to get. **many** ([legalclarity](https://legalclarity.org/bls-myheritage-ltd-charge-how-to-cancel-and-get-a-refund/), read 2026-10-09)

- A family site's manager lost the right to edit it. **one** (same)

**Lifestead must:** a suggested match is offered one at a time and never piles up; the person who made a tree never loses the right to edit it.

## FamilySearch (free family tree)

**Dislikes**
- One shared tree that anybody can edit, so unrelated people make wrong changes and FamilySearch steps in only in extreme cases. **many** ([FamilySearch Community](https://community.familysearch.org/en/discussion/122319/wrong-info-added), read 2026-10-09)

**Lifestead must:** the tree is the person's own; another person's change arrives as a suggestion shown before it is accepted.

## Things 3 (leader: personal task manager)

## Airthings (radon and air monitor)

**Dislikes**
- Monitors lose Wi-Fi and fail to hold a connection. **many** ([justuseapp](https://justuseapp.com/en/app/1560329275/airthings/reviews), read 2026-10-09)

## EWG Tap Water Database (water quality by utility)

**Dislikes**
- Water utilities call it alarmist: EWG compares results against health guidelines it set itself rather than the legal limits, and does not say in detail how those were derived. **several** ([City of Napa](https://www.cityofnapa.org/DocumentCenter/View/5930); [MWRA](https://www.mwra.com/node/4708), read 2026-10-09)

**Lifestead must:** water quality at home (second gap review) shows each reading against the legal limit and any stricter health guideline as two named lines, each with its source and tier, never one alarming colour.

## FEMA app (US emergency alerts)

4.3 from 5,420 ratings on Google Play. No dated complaints found beyond the alerts limit of five locations ([Computerworld](https://www.computerworld.com/article/2711060/storms-test-red-cross--tornado-app.html), read 2026-10-09).
