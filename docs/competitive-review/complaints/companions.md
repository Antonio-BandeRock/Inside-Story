# What users of the companion and caregiver competitors complain about

Researched 2026-10-09. Same format as `food.md`: dislikes, wishes (fix, change, add) and useless, each with source, date (the date read where the source gives none) and recurrence, ending with what Lifestead must do so the same complaint cannot be made of it.

Recurrence key: **many** (raised across many reviews or posters, or measured as a share of reviews), **several** (two to five people), **one** (a single person, kept because it is specific and checkable).

The same caution as `food.md` applies: justuseapp, Kimola, Unstar, appfollow, marlvel and appshunter are review-analysis or SEO sites, some of them competitors. Their percentages are reported as they claim them, not verified.

"Companions" here means the Partner, Household, Guardian and Caregiver tiers, which are designed and not built (CLAUDE.md, Monetization tiers; Q73 to Q90 in `docs/competitive-review/plan/items.txt`). Already covered elsewhere: Medisafe (Medfriend) in `schedules.md`. Apple Health (Sharing) and Apple Medical ID are parts of the phone, covered under `signals.md`, `schedules.md` and `insights.md`. "Nobody reviewed" is the placeholder `apps.json` uses for a function no competitor offers, so it has no section. ianacare and Caring Village had only praise and descriptions in what was found (ianacare's reviews come mostly through employers); OurHome had no dated complaints; all three are left for a later pass.

---

## Across the whole category first

1. **The connection is the thing paid for, and it is paywalled** (Paired: one teaser question a day, everything else locked). **many**
2. **What the helper writes does not reach the person** (Lotsa Helping Hands: comments on tasks show on the website only; reminders stopped). **several**
3. **Controls that silently stop holding** (Apple Screen Time limits reverting for days or weeks; over 2,300 people on one Apple thread). **many**
4. **A company deciding what a family's arrangement is** (Google emailing children near 13 about removing supervision, reversed within days). **many**

**What Lifestead must do across the board:** a caregiver link is paid by the cared-for person and costs the caregiver nothing (the tier table); everything written on either side of a link arrives on both, on every device, or says plainly that it has not yet; any setting one person makes for another is shown on both phones with when it last took effect, so a lapse is seen the same day; Lifestead never contacts a child or a cared-for person about changing their arrangement, and a Guardian link ends only by the parent's choice or by a step both see.

## Lotsa Helping Hands (leader: coordinating help from friends and family)

**Dislikes**
- Login refused with the right email and password, or "account already in use". **several** ([justuseapp](https://justuseapp.com/en/app/606923858/lotsa-helping-hands/contact), read 2026-10-09)
- Automatic task reminders stopped sending; no reply after 72 hours and several emails; no phone support. **one** (same)
- Comments on tasks appear only on the website, not in the app, "making the app completely useless". **several** (same)

**Lifestead must:** a helper's task, comment and reminder are the same on every device, and a helper needs no account of their own beyond the app on their phone and the invitation they accepted.

## Jointly (UK carers' coordination)

A one-off £2.99 to set up a circle for the person cared for, then nothing more for anyone invited ([App Store](https://apps.apple.com/lr/app/jointly/id560178300), read 2026-10-09). No dated complaints found; the pricing is the point worth keeping.

**Lifestead must:** match or beat a one-off price for one circle: the first caregiver comes with Individual, and nobody invited pays.

## Paired (leader: couples app)

**Dislikes**
- The free version shows one teaser question a day and locks quizzes, follow-ups, tips and nearly everything else. **many** ([Unstar](https://unstar.app/blog/paired-lasting-love-nudge-evergreen-cupla-couples-apps-ranked-2026), read 2026-10-09)
- High price, hard to cancel, unexpected charges. **many** ([Kimola](https://kimola.com/reports/unlock-insights-paired-app-customer-feedback-analysis-google-play-en-144872), read 2026-10-09)
- Prompts run out. **several** (Unstar)

**Lifestead must:** Partner is one price for both people, each with full Individual access, and nothing about the link itself is rationed.

## Google Family Link (leader: parental supervision on Android)

**Dislikes**
- Google emailed children approaching 13 about removing parental supervision, framed as a graduation; an online safety advocate called it "among the most predatory corporate practices", and Google reversed it within days so a teen needs a parent's approval. **many** ([Bitdefender](https://www.bitdefender.com/en-us/blog/hotforsecurity/google-backtracks-on-teen-parental-controls-after-public-backlash); [Al Jazeera, 2026-01-14](https://www.aljazeera.com/economy/2026/1/14/child-rights-org-says-google-undermines-parental-control-of-child-accounts), read 2026-10-09)

**Lifestead must:** the Guardian tier's handover to a child is the parent's pace, area by area, as decided; the app never prompts a child to leave it.

## Apple Family Sharing (Screen Time for children)

**Dislikes**
- Downtime schedules reverted or stopped applying, unnoticed for days or weeks; Apple admitted the bug, said iOS 16.5 fixed it, and it persisted into iOS 17. **many** ([9to5Mac, 2023-07-31](https://9to5mac.com/2023/07/31/screen-time-is-broken/); [channelnews](https://www.channelnews.com.au/apple-admits-theres-a-bug-in-screen-time-parental-controls/)); over 2,300 people on one Apple discussion page. **many**

**Lifestead must:** the across-the-board line on settings made for another person showing when they last took effect.
