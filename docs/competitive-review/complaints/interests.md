# What users of the Interests competitors complain about

Researched 2026-10-09. Same format as `food.md`: dislikes, wishes (fix, change, add) and useless, each with source, date (the date read where the source gives none) and recurrence, ending with what Lifestead must do so the same complaint cannot be made of it.

Recurrence key: **many** (raised across many reviews or posters, or measured as a share of reviews), **several** (two to five people), **one** (a single person, kept because it is specific and checkable).

The same caution as `food.md` applies: justuseapp, Kimola, Unstar, appfollow, marlvel and appshunter are review-analysis or SEO sites, some of them competitors. Their percentages are reported as they claim them, not verified. nibble-app sells a study app of its own, so read it the same way.

The Interests tab and its Learn lens are not built (CLAUDE.md items 31 and 37), so every "Lifestead must" below is a requirement for when they are. SCORE and the SBA Learning Center are free mentoring and reading from US bodies with no review channel; Making It Pay cites them. "Duolingo widget" is part of Duolingo and is under it.

---

## Across the whole category first

1. **What was free became paid once people depended on it** (Quizlet's Learn and Test modes in 2022, Trello's ten-collaborator cap in 2024, QuickBooks Self-Employed closed to new users and its mileage tracking left to stop). **many**
2. **Guilt used to keep people coming back** (Duolingo's streaks kept at midnight through illness and grief, the sad owl, passive-aggressive reminders). **many**
3. **Cloud-first tools that fail without a signal** (Notion offline, slow on mobile). **many**
4. **A feed filled with things nobody chose** (Pinterest's AI images, and a control to see fewer that does not work). **many**
5. **Steep starts with no guidance inside the app** (Anki's interface and community tutorials). **many**

**What Lifestead must do across the board:** a deck, a project or an interest made on Free stays usable on Free (Learn's ready-made decks are Free by decision, making your own is paid, and nothing crosses that line later); no streak, no count of days missed, no sad mascot, no reminder that scolds (the item 29 and C17 rules); everything works offline because the record is on the phone; nothing appears in Interests that the person did not add or ask for; a first deck or project is started by a guided step inside the app.

## Anki (leader: spaced-repetition flashcards)

**Dislikes**
- An old, unclear interface and a steep start that leans on community tutorials rather than help inside the app. **many** ([Unstar](https://unstar.app/blog/quizlet-anki-brainscape-knowt-studysmarter-flashcard-study-apps-ranked-2026), read 2026-10-09; [G2](https://www.g2.com/survey_responses/anki-review-12846625))
- Free on desktop, Android and web, but $24.99 on iPhone, which catches people out. **many** ([nibble-app](https://nibble-app.com/blog/anki-review), read 2026-10-09)

**Liked:** the scheduling works, and the decks are the person's to keep. **many** (same)

**Lifestead must:** Learn's spaced repetition is as sound as Anki's, a first deck is made in a guided step, the price is the same on every platform, and decks import from and export to Anki's format.

## Quizlet (leader: study sets)

**Dislikes**
- On 2022-08-01 Learn and Test moved behind Quizlet Plus; free users get five rounds of Learn and one practice test per set. **many** ([Spartan Shield](https://spartanshield.org/33765/opinion/new-year-new-frustrations-quizlet-switches-to-payment-based-plans/); [NT Daily](https://www.ntdaily.com/opinion/quizlet-s-paywalls-place-priority-on-profits-over-pupils/article_848d54c6-4eca-11ef-b6bd-bba8eff900d3.html), read 2026-10-09)
- $35.99 a year on the web and $44.99 on iPhone for the same plan. **several** ([rapidevelopers](https://rapidevelopers.com/clone/quizlet))
- Students blocked part way through studying. **many** ([Bloomington South Optimist](https://bloomingtonsouthoptimist.org/17407/opinion/quizlets-paywall-hurts-the-students-it-claims-to-help))

**Lifestead must:** a ready-made deck on Free is studied in every mode, with no round count; the price is the same wherever it is paid.

## Duolingo (leader: language learning; also the Duolingo widget)

**Dislikes**
- The free plan is cluttered with ads and rationed by energy. **many** ([Unstar](https://unstar.app/pt-BR/blog/duolingo-babbel-rosetta-stone-busuu-memrise-language-learning-apps-ranked-2026), read 2026-10-09)
- Streaks kept at midnight through travel, illness and grief; a missed day felt out of proportion. **many** ([University XP, 2025-07-25](https://www.universityxp.com/news/2025/7/25/we-havent-seen-you-in-a-while-duolingos-passive-aggressive-strategy-for-keeping-users-hooked); [Cursor](https://cursor.tue.nl/en/opinion/alain-starke/guilt-tripped-by-an-owl))
- The owl on the widget and in the app is annoying. **several** (same)
- The "AI first" announcement in April 2025, contractors replaced, walked back within a week after the backlash. **many** ([tech.co](https://tech.co/news/duolingo-ai-layoffs); [Computing, 2025](https://www.computing.co.uk/news/2025/ai/duolingo-goes-ai-first-as-it-phases-out-human-contractors))
- "Duolingo is deteriorating fast". **many** ([r/duolingo, 2024-12-17](https://lr.psf.lt/r/duolingo/comments/1hgj15n/duolingo_is_deteriorating_fast/m2om0l7/?context=3))

**Lifestead must:** Inside Knowledge on Home shows what the person now knows, a count that only grows, and never a streak or a missed day; no energy, no hearts, no ads; every deck says where its content came from.

## Notion (leader: personal workspace and projects)

**Dislikes**
- Offline is unreliable; it does not work well without a steady connection. **many** ([Capterra](https://www.capterra.com/p/186596/Notion/reviews/?rating=1), read 2026-10-09)
- Mobile is slower, does less and is harder to use than desktop. **many** (same)
- Slow pages, lag in large workspaces, freezing. **many** ([Unstar](https://unstar.app/blog/notion-evernote-obsidian-apple-notes-onenote-note-taking-apps-ranked-2026), read 2026-10-09)

**Lifestead must:** Projects (item 31) work fully on the phone with no signal and are as quick on the phone as on the computer.

## Trello (leader: boards for projects)

**Dislikes**
- From 2024-04-08 a free workspace holds ten collaborators, pending invitations counted, and past that every board turns view-only. **many** ([Atlassian Community](https://community.atlassian.com/forums/Trello-articles/Update-on-Collaborator-Limit-for-Free-Trello-Workspaces/bc-p/2632035); [GIGAZINE, 2024-03-05](https://www.gigazine.net/gsc_news/en/20240305-trello-free-plan-collaborator-limits))

**Lifestead must:** a limit is never enforced by turning someone's own work read only; a project shared with others stays editable by its owner whatever the tier.

## Pinterest (leader: saving ideas)

**Dislikes**
- AI images fill the feed, AI moderation pulls posts and bans accounts, and hand-drawn art gets labelled as AI. **many** ([404 Media](https://404media.co/pinterest-is-drowning-in-a-sea-of-ai-slop-and-auto-moderation), read 2026-10-09)
- The setting to see fewer AI pins is on and they still come: of 85 reviews mentioning the setting, 63 reported it failing. **many** ([Unstar](https://unstar.app/blog/pinterest-ai-filter-not-working-see-less-ai-reviews-2026); [TechCrunch, 2025-10-16](https://techcrunch.com/2025/10/16/pinterest-adds-controls-to-let-you-limit-the-amount-of-ai-slop-in-your-feed/?sidebar=a))

**Lifestead must:** Share to Inside Story saves the page the person chose, with its address and the day, and there is no feed.

## QuickBooks Solopreneur (also: QuickBooks Self-Employed; leader: self-employed bookkeeping)

3.9 from about 100 reviews on Software Advice.

**Dislikes**
- Wrong mileage figures; Etsy and PayPal imports inconsistent; bank connections fail; poor support. **several** ([Software Advice](https://softwareadvice.com/accounting/quickbooks-self-employed-profile/reviews/), read 2026-10-09)
- Self-Employed closed to new sign-ups and its support ended, and automatic mileage tracking stopped working. **many** ([r/QuickBooks, 2024-11-12](https://redlib.ssps.io/r/QuickBooks/comments/1gptqvq/the_fall_of_qbse/lwwwz39/?context=3); [Merchant Maverick](https://www.merchantmaverick.com/reviews/quickbooks-self-employed-review/))

**Lifestead must:** Making It Pay keeps income and costs as the person's own records, importable from a CSV and exportable to one, so no product change can strand a year's figures, and it never advises starting a business or leaving a job.
