# What users of the Signals competitors complain about

Researched 2026-10-09. Same format as `food.md`: dislikes, wishes (fix, change, add) and useless, each with source, date (the date read where the source gives none) and recurrence, ending with what Lifestead must do so the same complaint cannot be made of it.

Recurrence key: **many** (raised across many reviews or posters, or measured as a share of reviews), **several** (two to five people), **one** (a single person, kept because it is specific and checkable).

The same caution as `food.md` applies: justuseapp, Kimola, Unstar, appfollow, marlvel and appshunter are review-analysis or SEO sites, some of them competitors. Their percentages are reported as they claim them, not verified.

Already covered elsewhere: CareClinic, Oura and Strava in `schedules.md`; Monash FODMAP (and its reintroduction) in `food.md`. "Apple Health and Google Health" and "Flo and Clue" are composites of apps listed by name. "Bearable experiments" and "Bearable Premium" are parts of Bearable, so they sit in its section. "Zoe" and "ZOE" are one app.

---

## Across the whole category first

1. **Logging every day for months and learning nothing new.** Said of mySymptoms, Visible and Bearable in nearly the same words. This is the category's deepest failure and the one Lifestead's core purpose is aimed at. **many**
2. **The pattern-finding is the part behind the paywall** (Bearable's correlations, Visible's pacing insights, Clue beyond the calendar), so a person logs for free and pays to learn what the log says. **many**
3. **Billing: trials that turn into annual charges, cancelling that routes nowhere, charges after cancelling** (Calm, Headspace, ZOE, Bearable, Viome, Thorne). **many**
4. **Privacy of the most intimate data**: Flo shared period and pregnancy intentions with Facebook and settled with the FTC; Euki is praised by Mozilla precisely for keeping everything on the device. **many**
5. **Tags and choices written for somebody healthier**: Visible's "go to work" and "take a walk" for people who are bedbound; Cara Care's missing conditions. **several**

**What Lifestead must do across the board:** Pattern Finder and Trends say what a person's records show, with what they are counted against, and say plainly when there is not enough yet rather than offering nothing new as if it were a finding; nothing leaves the device without the person sending it; cancelling is one step inside the app; every list of tags or choices takes the person's own (the open-lists rule), and the built-in ones include what a bad day holds (rest, lying down, a shower counted as effort).

## Bearable (leader: symptom and mood log, correlations, experiments)

4.8 from about 5,300 ratings. Premium runs from $4.49 a month to $34.99 a year depending on country ([Bearable](https://bearable.app/symptom-tracker/), read 2026-10-09).

**Dislikes**
- Billing friction is the top driver of negative reviews, described as a "rigmarole" ending in accidental charges. **many** ([marlvel](https://marlvel.ai/apps/bearable-symptom-tracker), read 2026-10-09)
- Correlations and impacts are Premium only, so the reason to log is paid. **several** (same; [Adapty paywall library](https://adapty.io/paywall-library/bearable-symptom-tracker/))
- The whole setup is gone through before it says only a trial is free. **one** ([appfollow](https://apps.appfollow.io/ios/bearable-symptom-med-tracker/1482581097?country=ph))

**Wishes**
- Add: sync with the Oura ring and MyFitnessPal. **several** (same)

**Lifestead must:** say what is Free before setup starts, not after; Pattern Finder's candidates, with their denominators, are what decides whether logging was worth it, so where it sits on the free/paid board is decided with that in mind; read Oura through Health Connect or Apple Health.

## Visible (leader: pacing for ME/CFS, long Covid, POTS)

4.8 from 325 ratings; uses a Polar Verity Sense band; Visible Plus is paid on top of the band.

**Dislikes**
- Heart-rate alerts at resting plus 15 fire during eating and washing, so they become noise. **several** ([Science for ME forum, 2023-07-23](https://s4me.info/threads/monitoring-app-visible-a-platform-designed-for-any-invisible-illness-that-benefits-from-resting-and-pacing-including-me-cfs-long-covid.23542/page-4))
- The HRV score bore no relation to symptoms or capacity. **one** (same, 2023-09-28)
- Measuring HRV at the same time each day is hard with cognitive limits. **one** (same)
- Activity tags such as "go to work" do not fit bedbound users. **one** (same)
- Nearly £100 a year to connect the band. **one** (same, 2023-09-23)
- After learning a baseline, "rarely, if ever" told anything new. **one** (same)
- The band loosens and needs tightening. **several** ([appfollow](https://apps.appfollow.io/ios/visible-pacing-for-illness/1624474919?country=au))

**Lifestead must:** any alert threshold is the person's to set and says what it is built from; a score is never shown that the person cannot check against their own symptom log beside it (Compare Two does this); a measurement that has to be taken at the same time each day is a reminder, never a lost day when missed.

## Guava (leader: medical records and labs in one place)

66.7% positive by justuseapp's reading of 773 reviews. Praised for reading lab PDFs into charts and for fixing bugs within days.

**Wishes**
- Add: a list of the person's providers with their contact details. **several** ([justuseapp](https://justuseapp.com/en/app/1622255863/guava-health-tracker/reviews), read 2026-10-09)
- Add: a watch and widgets for quick entries such as a dose or a glass of water. **one** (same)
- Add: local weather alongside symptoms. **one** (same)
- Change: connecting every patient portal at once left a lot of clean-up. **one** (same)
- Logging still takes too long. **one** (same)

**Lifestead must:** providers are contacts with roles (item 34, Contacts); weather recorded beside each flare; an import shows what will come in and lets the person leave parts out before anything is written.

## mySymptoms Food Diary (leader: food and symptom diary)

justuseapp reads its reviews as 60.5% negative.

**Dislikes**
- Months of daily logging "hasn't been able to tell them anything they don't already know". **one**, and the same as Visible's and the category's ([justuseapp](https://justuseapp.com/en/app/405231632/mysymptoms-food-diary/reviews), read 2026-10-09)
- A clumsy interface; the developers do not answer. **several** (same)

**Lifestead must:** see the category line above; Pattern Finder's "test this" experiment gives a person a way to find out rather than wait.

## Cara Care (also: IBS food and symptom diary)

justuseapp reads its reviews as 67.9% negative.

**Dislikes**
- Will not open on newer Samsung and Android phones. **many** ([Kimola, Google Play](https://kimola.com/reports/unlock-insights-cara-care-app-feedback-analysis-report-google-play-en-ca-156660))
- Several foods cannot be logged at once. **several** ([Kimola, App Store](https://kimola.com/reports/insightful-cara-care-app-review-analysis-app-store-us-156661))
- The person's condition is not in the list. **several** (same)
- Sync, privacy and support worries. **several** (same)

**Lifestead must:** a meal is many foods in one entry (built); conditions beyond the 19 can be followed under Other Conditions.

## Nerva (also: gut-directed hypnotherapy for IBS)

4.3 on Trustpilot from 1,583 reviews.

**Dislikes**
- Five-minute delays responding to a tap. **several** ([justuseapp](https://justuseapp.com/en/app/1467398796/nerva-ibs-gut-hypnotherapy/problems))
- Finished sessions not marked as done. **several** (same)
- $75 for three months of recordings that have not changed in three years, which people feel they now depend on. **one** ([Trustpilot](https://nz.trustpilot.com/review/try.nervaibs.com))
- Hard selling; a narrator's pace that works against relaxing. **several** (same)

**Lifestead must:** a recording or programme bought once stays the person's; finishing is recorded the moment it happens.

## ZOE (also: gut health and food scores)

**Dislikes**
- People did not realise they had started an ongoing subscription and could not cancel; only automated replies. **many** ([Trustpilot](https://uk.trustpilot.com/review/zoe.com?page=5))
- £9.99 a month charged for about 18 months after cancelling. **one** (same)
- No way to reach the company outside the app. **several** (same)

**Lifestead must:** cancelling is one step in the app and its confirmation shows on screen; the subscription screen names what renews, when, and for how much.

## Viome (also: microbiome test and supplements)

About 3 of 5 on Trustpilot from about 3,000 reviews; 1.24 from 59 at the BBB.

**Dislikes**
- Results felt like a route to selling expensive supplements. **many** ([Trustpilot](https://www.trustpilot.com/review/viome.com?page=10); [Medical News Today](https://www.medicalnewstoday.com/articles/viome))
- Samples rejected, results late, refunds hard. **many** (same)
- Results not detailed or readable enough. **several** ([Kimola](https://kimola.com/reports/unlock-insights-viome-customer-feedback-analysis-trustpilot-en-us-146403))

**Lifestead must:** sell nothing that it also recommends; food first, a supplement only for the gap, never a product.

## Thorne (also: supplement records)

Complaints are about the company more than an app: calls and emails unanswered, subscriptions and stored cards hard to remove. **several** ([Trustpilot](https://uk.trustpilot.com/review/www.thorne.com?page=4)). **Lifestead must:** store no card; nothing to remove.

## Clue (leader: cycle tracking)

**Dislikes**
- Only the calendar is free; everything else pays, with more Clue+ pop-ups interrupting a log. **many** ([unitQ](https://unitq.com/unitq-scorecards/clueperiodcycletracker))
- Predictions wrong for irregular cycles (19% of period-tracker complaints). **many** ([Unstar, 2026](https://unstar.app/fr/blog/flo-clue-stardust-apple-health-period-tracking-apps-ranked-2026))
- Privacy fears are 28% of period-tracker complaints across the category. **many** (same)

**Lifestead must:** cycle tracking (item 25) shows a range and says how sure it is, widening for an irregular cycle; no pop-ups inside a log.

## Flo (also: cycle tracking)

**Dislikes**
- Shared when a person had her period or intended to get pregnant with Facebook and analytics firms despite promising not to; FTC settlement, then class actions with Meta, Google and Flurry ($3.5 million). **many** ([Courthouse News](https://www.courthousenews.com/flo-settles-data-privacy-suit-over-menstrual-tracking-app/); [Bloomberg Law](https://news.bloomberglaw.com/privacy-and-data-security/defunct-analytics-firm-pens-privacy-deal-over-period-tracker))

**Lifestead must:** no analytics or advertising code in the app at all, which is checkable by anyone reading the build.

## Euki (also: private cycle tracking)

Not a complaint but the bar: Mozilla recommends it because data stays on the device, behind a PIN, and can be wiped at once ([Mozilla Foundation](https://www.mozillafoundation.org/en/nothing-personal/euki-privacy-review/)). **Lifestead must:** match it (local-first and App Lock are built) and add a wipe that a person can find in under a minute.

## Daylio (also: mood and activity log)

**Dislikes**
- Aggressive subscription prompts; core features and automatic backup paywalled; past entries hard to reach without paying. **many** ([Kimola, Google Play](https://kimola.com/reports/in-depth-daylio-app-feedback-report-now-available-google-play-en-us-141927))
- An entry made without going through the notification was not saved. **one** (same)

**Wishes**
- Add: short videos, more than three photos, bulk photo pick. **several** (same)

**Lifestead must:** backup to the person's own drive is never paid; a person's past records are never locked away from them; an entry saves the same however it is started.

## Day One (leader: journal)

**Dislikes**
- Sync across devices is paid and still fails: entries missing, conflicts, web and app out of step; one outage put entries in other people's journals. **many** ([Unstar, 2026](https://unstar.app/blog/day-one-journey-reflectly-stoic-finch-journaling-apps-ranked-2026); [Mac Power Users forum](https://talk.macpowerusers.com/t/absolutely-fed-up-with-day-one-warning-rant/16237?page=2))
- AI prompts paywalled; export friction; nagging after updates; a "Day One Gold" upsell. **many** (same; [Day One forum](https://forums.dayoneapp.com/forums/topic/disgraceful-upsell-upgrade-to-day-one-gold-looking-for-alternatives/))

**Lifestead must:** the Diary (item 32) syncs through the person's own folder, never a company server, so no outage can put an entry in somebody else's hands; export in an open format at any time.

## Apple Journal (also: journal)

## Journey (also: journal)

**Dislikes**

## Calm and Headspace (also: calm and rest)

Ratings 4.37 and 4.45 on Google Play in July 2026; the one-star reviews are about money, not the content.

**Dislikes**
- Free trial ends in a full annual charge with no warning; refund window already closed. **many** ([Unstar, 2026](https://unstar.app/blog/calm-headspace-insight-timer-balance-ten-percent-happier-meditation-apps-ranked-2026))
- Cancelling hidden behind menus that route to the store. **many** (same)
- Calm still charging after cancelling. **several** ([Kimola, Trustpilot](https://kimola.com/reports/calm-app-feedback-analysis-insights-into-user-dissatisfaction-trustpilot-en-us-155924))
- The free meditation from the advert turns out to need a subscription. **many** (Unstar, same)
- Audio stops mid-session; offline downloads fail. **several** (same)

**Lifestead must:** a reminder before any trial ends, with the date and amount; anything shown in a store listing as Free is Free; anything offered for a bad day works offline.
