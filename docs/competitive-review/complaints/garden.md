# What users of the Garden competitors complain about

Researched 2026-10-09. Same format as `food.md`: dislikes, wishes (fix, change, add) and useless, each with source, date (the date read where the source gives none) and recurrence, ending with what Lifestead must do so the same complaint cannot be made of it.

Recurrence key: **many** (raised across many reviews or posters, or measured as a share of reviews), **several** (two to five people), **one** (a single person, kept because it is specific and checkable).

The same caution as `food.md` applies: justuseapp, Kimola, Unstar, appfollow, marlvel and appshunter are review-analysis or SEO sites, some of them competitors. Their percentages are reported as they claim them, not verified.

Already covered elsewhere: Gardenize, Planter, Seedtime and GrowVeg in `trends.md`. "BBKA guides" and "Garden Organic and extension services" are reading from associations and universities with no review channel; Lifestead's crop guides already cite them. Seed Keeper turned up only an unrelated password app of the same name, GrowDiaries only descriptions of its community, and Preserve It no app of that name; all three are left for a later pass.

---

## Across the whole category first

1. **Identification stated more surely than it is** (PictureThis, Planta, Seek, Pl@ntNet, Merlin, RHS Grow): wrong plants, birds and fungi, and care advice that follows the wrong answer. **many**
2. **Paywalls in front of the first use** (PictureThis' three screens with a muted x, Planta's scan, RHS Grow's paywall after download, Veglog's $69.99 after a free offer). **many**
3. **A record that cannot move**: Seek observations missing from iNaturalist, nothing carried to a new phone, Apiary Book's backup not automatic, BeePlus photos disappearing, 11pets locking pet records behind a new subscription. **many**
4. **Fixed lists with no add-your-own** (From Seed to Spoon missing guava, with no way to add a plant). **several**
5. **Reminders on a clock that do not notice what was done** (Planta's watering schedule not moving when watered early or late, read as causing overwatering). **several**

**What Lifestead must do across the board:** an identification from the camera is a suggestion with how sure it is, the next likely answers beside it, and care advice that waits until the person confirms which plant it is; nothing in Garden is behind a screen before first use; every Garden record travels in the encrypted copy and in an open export, and nothing already recorded is ever put behind a new payment; every list takes the person's own entries (the open-lists rule); a care reminder resets from when the thing was last done, not from when it was due.

## PictureThis (leader: plant identification)

**Dislikes**
- Three screens selling the paid version on opening, the first two with no way past but a large Continue, the third with a small muted x. **many** ([deceptive.design](https://deceptive.design/articles/picture-this-app-uses-deceptive-tactics-to-trick-users-into-paid-upgrades-and-makes-it-difficult-to-cancel-subscriptions), read 2026-10-09)
- A free trial that became a $39.99 charge, with cancelling left to the person to find. **many** ([justuseapp](https://justuseapp.com/en/app/1252497129/picturethis-fleurs-et-arbres/reviews), read 2026-10-09)
- Identifications far off, above all for unusual and indoor plants. **several** ([Laidback Gardener, 2020-09-13](https://laidbackgardener.blog/2020/09/13/does-the-picturethis-plant-identifier-really-work/?amp=1))

**Lifestead must:** the across-the-board line; Garden opens on the person's own areas, never on an offer.

## Planta (leader: plant care reminders)

**Dislikes**
- About a quarter of reviewers felt misled about cost; scanning a plant needs payment. **many** ([Kimola](https://kimola.com/reports/comprehensive-planta-app-customer-feedback-report-app-store-us-155863), read 2026-10-09)
- The watering schedule leads to overwatering, and reminders do not shift when a plant was watered early or late (about one in five reviews). **many** (same)
- A pineapple plant named as a spider plant, so the care advice was useless. **one** ([Kimola, Google Play](https://kimola.com/reports/in-depth-planta-app-review-analysis-for-enhanced-user-satisfaction-google-play-156460))

**Lifestead must:** watering is recorded, and the next one is worked out from it and from the area's readings and rain (Growing Conditions), with the reason shown.

## iNaturalist (leader: nature observation and community identification)

justuseapp reads 33.5% of its reviews as negative.

**Dislikes**
- The new app is less clear than the old one, more cluttered, crashes when posting, and uploads one at a time. **many** ([justuseapp](https://justuseapp.com/en/app/6475737561/inaturalist/reviews), read 2026-10-09; [iNaturalist forum](https://forum.inaturalist.org/tag/mobile-app))
- No photo editing on iPhone. **several** (same)

**Liked:** a community that checks identifications. **many** (same)

**Lifestead must:** a sighting saved in Garden can be sent on to iNaturalist through its published interface, so the community check stays where it belongs.

## Seek by iNaturalist (nature identification for beginners)

**Dislikes**
- Weak on amphibians and fungi, and often only a broad guess from a good photo. **many** ([Kimola](https://kimola.com/reports/in-depth-seek-by-inaturalist-review-analysis-insights-feedback-google-play-en-140322), read 2026-10-09)
- Observations do not reliably reach iNaturalist; uploading is unclear. **many** ([iNaturalist forum](https://forum.inaturalist.org/t/feedback-for-seek-by-inaturalist/67176), read 2026-10-09)
- Observations lost when changing phones, with little support. **several** ([complaintsboard](https://www.complaintsboard.com/seek-by-inaturalist-b148883))

**Lifestead must:** a broad guess is labelled as the level it reached (genus, family), never dressed as a species.

## Pl@ntNet (plant identification, research project)

**Dislikes**
- Asks for access to all photos after the person chose selected photos only. **several** ([Unstar](https://unstar.app/blog/plant-identification-apps-ranked-picturethis-plantin-plantnet-2026), read 2026-10-09)
- A thinner mobile interface and slower answers. **several** (same)
- Apps of its kind are weak on rare and local plants and on plants without a flower. **many** ([AlgorithmWatch](https://algorithmwatch.org/en/plant-identifying-apps/))

**Liked:** quick, accurate separation of similar plants in Wirecutter's testing. **one** test (Unstar)

**Lifestead must:** ask only for the photo being used, never the whole library.

## RHS Grow Your Own (also: RHS Grow; UK growing advice)

3.0 from 62 reviews in the UK.

**Dislikes**
- A paywall found only after downloading (about 30% of reviews). **many** ([onclarity](https://www.onclarity.com/leaderboard/insight/rhsgrow), read 2026-10-09)
- Plant identification often wrong (about 20%). **several** (same)

**Liked:** advice from RHS experts, with links to the fuller RHS pages. **several** ([Gardens Illustrated](https://www.gardensillustrated.com/reviews/rhs-grow-app))

**Lifestead must:** keep citing RHS and the extension services beside every crop guide, as it does, and say what is Free before setup.

## Merlin Bird ID (leader: bird identification by sound and photo)

**Dislikes**
- Mimics and call notes misnamed: a Blue Jay as a Red-shouldered Hawk, a Starling as a hawk, kestrel or pewee, Ladder-backed Woodpeckers as Downy every time, a squirrel as a Steller's Jay. **many** ([Tweeters list](https://mailman1.u.washington.edu/pipermail/tweeters/2026-February/008350.html); [Bangor Daily News, 2026-06-13](https://www.bangordailynews.com/2026/06/13/outdoors/outdoors-contributors/merlin-birding-app-problems-joam40zk0w/))

**Liked:** accurate on a bird's typical song, and it says plainly its suggestions must be checked. **many** (same)

**Lifestead must:** take Merlin's habit of saying a suggestion needs checking, and a pollinator or bird count in Garden records what the person saw, not what an app guessed.

## 11pets (leader: pet care records)

**Dislikes**
- A new subscription locked long-time users out of their pets' records, and more than one pet now needs payment. **many** ([Kimola](https://kimola.com/reports/in-depth-analysis-of-11pets-pet-care-user-feedback-app-store-us-150767), read 2026-10-09)
- Data loss and login trouble after updates; slow, cluttered and expensive. **many** ([justuseapp](https://justuseapp.com/en/app/1232470530/11pets-pet-care/reviews), read 2026-10-09)

**Lifestead must:** a pet's records, once made, stay readable and exportable on every tier, and the number of animals is never what a price is counted by.

## HiveTracks (leader: beekeeping records)

**Dislikes**
- The mobile app does little next to the web version; login failures, account creation trouble and sync problems. **several** ([Kimola](https://kimola.com/reports/unlock-insights-with-our-hive-feedback-analysis-report-app-store-us-140334), read 2026-10-09)
- A beekeeper's long-standing objection to how it boxes the beekeeper into its way of recording. **one** ([Honey Bee Suite](https://www.honeybeesuite.com/%e2%80%9chive-tracks%e2%80%9d-boxes-the-beekeeper/))

**Lifestead must:** the hive log (not built) works fully on the phone at the hive, with no account and no signal needed, and takes the beekeeper's own fields.

## Apiary Book (beekeeping records)

**Dislikes**
- Complicated with no tutorial; non-US date formats a struggle; a cap on hives in the free version. **several** ([chrome-stats](https://chrome-stats.com/d/com.csg.apiarybook), read 2026-10-09)
- Cluttered and not usable with gloves on. **several** (same)
- Backup is not automatic, so records can be lost. **several** (same)

**Liked:** tracking many hives and apiaries, reminders, a free hobbyist tier, responsive support. **several** (same)

**Lifestead must:** hive inspection buttons large enough for a gloved finger, voice entry at the hive, dates in the phone's own format, and no cap on the number of hives.

## BeePlus (beekeeping manager)

4.68 from 824 ratings in the US.

**Dislikes**
- Photos disappear from inspections; moving the data elsewhere is hard. **several** ([marlvel](https://marlvel.ai/apps/uk-co-omnichrome-beeplus), read 2026-10-09)
- No way to contact support from the website. **one** (same)

**Lifestead must:** an inspection photo is stored with its record and travels in the export.

## Farmbrite (small farm management)

4.8 from 62 reviews on Capterra.

**Dislikes**
- Navigation, search and filtering livestock are awkward; some entry is tedious. **several** ([Capterra](https://www.capterra.com/p/136765/Farmbrite/reviews/?page=3), read 2026-10-09)

**Liked:** one place for livestock, crops, money and tasks; human support. **many** (same)

**Lifestead must:** Garden's records (areas, plantings, animals, hives, costs, harvests) sit in one search, the way Where did I put it already searches places.

## Veglog (also: Garden Planner: VegLog)

justuseapp reads 69.4% of 48 reviews as negative.

**Dislikes**
- Charged $69.99 after being offered a free account. **several** ([justuseapp](https://justuseapp.com/en/app/6444013681/garden-planner-veglog/reviews), read 2026-10-09)
- No growing guidance, no planting suggestions by temperature, no watering or feeding reminders. **several** (same)

**Lifestead must:** sowing windows come from the area's location and its recorded readings, and every planting can raise its own care reminders.

## From Seed to Spoon (garden planner)

justuseapp reads 33.3% of reviews as negative.

**Dislikes**
- Photos do not load in GrowBot; unexpected $4.99 subscription charges. **several** ([justuseapp](https://justuseapp.com/en/app/1312538762/from-seed-to-spoon-gardening/reviews), read 2026-10-09)
- Common plants such as guava missing, with no way to add one. **several** (same)

**Lifestead must:** any crop can be added by the person (the open-lists rule), and it then works everywhere a built-in crop does.

## Ball Canning (leader: home canning recipes)

**Dislikes**
- Iowa State University found Ball's canning instructions short of current standards, with recipes lacking testing data or exact measurements. **several** ([Tasting Table](https://www.tastingtable.com/1084425/why-the-ball-blue-book-is-controversial-in-the-canned-food-world), read 2026-10-09)

**Lifestead must:** preserving the harvest (second gap review) uses only tested recipes and times from USDA and the National Center for Home Food Preservation, with the processing time adjusted for altitude, and names the source on every recipe.

## Seed Savers Exchange (heirloom seed exchange and catalogue)

TrustScore 2.5 on Trustpilot; the complaints are about orders rather than an app.

**Dislikes**
- Orders marked delivered that never came; dead plants and mouldy seed. **several** ([Trustpilot](https://nl-be.trustpilot.com/review/www.seedsavers.org), read 2026-10-09)

**Lifestead must:** saved seed (second gap review) records where each lot came from, its year and a germination test, so a person knows what they have before sowing it.
