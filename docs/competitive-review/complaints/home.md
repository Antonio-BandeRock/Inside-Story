# What users of the Home competitors complain about

Researched 2026-10-09. Same format as `food.md`: dislikes, wishes (fix, change, add) and useless, each with source, date (the date read where the source gives none) and recurrence, ending with what Lifestead must do so the same complaint cannot be made of it.

Recurrence key: **many** (raised across many reviews or posters, or measured as a share of reviews), **several** (two to five people), **one** (a single person, kept because it is specific and checkable).

The same caution as `food.md` applies: justuseapp, Kimola, Unstar, appfollow, marlvel and appshunter are review-analysis or SEO sites, some of them competitors. Their percentages are reported as they claim them, not verified.

The Reports tab named no app of its own in `apps.json`, so it has no file; its competitors (Apple Health sharing, Bearable's export, CareClinic's report) are covered where they were first named.

Already covered elsewhere: Daylio (Year in Pixels is part of it) in `signals.md`. "Apple Health (Pinned)" is a part of Apple Health, whose complaints are in `signals.md` (Health Records) and `schedules.md` (Medications, hydration). "Health websites and national health services" is a composite with no single review channel. Healthline is a website first; its reviews are about its shop and discounts rather than its reading, so it has no section. Life Calendar's original app was discontinued and the apps now using the name had no dated complaints; left for a later pass.

## Across the whole category first

1. **The daily-living audience is billed the way an inattentive person is most likely to miss** (Goblin.tools: "a free trial that users may forget to cancel due to ADHD"; Headway; Noom's "difficult by design" cancellation). **many**
2. **Notes that vanish.** Apple Notes showing zero notes after a new iCloud terms prompt; Google Keep losing data after updates. **many**
3. **Export that loses the content** (Apple Notes' export dropping formatting and attachments). **several**
4. **A tool that says what to do and then does not help do it**: Goblin.tools breaks a task down with no timer, reminder or schedule. **several**

**What Lifestead must do across the board:** no free trial that converts silently, a reminder two days before any first charge, and cancelling in one step inside the app (the same line as `signals.md`, said twice because this audience is the one it costs most); a capture or note is in the database the moment it is saved and nothing a sync or terms prompt does can show an empty list in its place; export keeps photos and recordings beside the text; every breakdown of a task can become a routine with its reminders in one press.

## Finch (leader: self-care companion)

4.9 on the App Store; the 1 to 3 star reviews are about subscriptions, permissions and billing ([Autonomous review](https://www.autonomous.ai/en-GB/ourblog/finch-self-care-app-review-full-breakdown), read 2026-10-09). No dated complaint found beyond that; left for a later pass.

**Lifestead must:** Finch's warmth comes from a pet that grows with care. Lifestead's rule is that nothing is awarded for records (item 29), so the warmth has to come from what the person's own records show, said kindly, and from Playful Wording off health screens.

## Goblin.tools (leader: task breakdown for ADHD)

justuseapp reads 66.6% of 303 reviews as negative, mostly about the app rather than the free website.

**Dislikes**
- Answering setup questions only to be told it needs a subscription, with a trial "users may forget to cancel due to ADHD". **many** ([justuseapp](https://justuseapp.com/en/app/6479981873/goblin-tools-adhd-planner/reviews), read 2026-10-09)
- Not told before downloading that only seven days were free. **several** (same)
- Repeated review prompts during setup. **several** (same)
- It solves "what should I do" but has no timer, reminders or scheduling to follow through. **many** ([saner.ai](https://www.saner.ai/blogs/goblin-tools-review))

**Liked:** the breakdown gives immediate relief from an overwhelming task. **many** (same)

**Lifestead must:** say what is Free before setup; ask for a review never during setup and at most once; a breakdown becomes a Routine with timed steps and reminders, and The one next thing (always Free) shows its first step.

## Noom (leader: behaviour-change weight loss)

**Dislikes**
- A $62 million class action settlement ($56 million cash) over "risk-free" trials that locked people into renewals. **many** ([Winston & Strawn](https://www.winston.com/en/class-action-insider/noom-settlement-showcases-potential-pitfalls-on-auto-renewals.html), read 2026-10-09)
- Cancelling meant messaging a coach bot that pointed to the app store; a former engineer called it "difficult by design". **many** (same; [athletechnews](https://athletechnews.com/noom-class-action-settlement/))
- Over 2,000 BBB complaints by 2020. **many** ([Subscription Insider](https://www.subscriptioninsider.com/audience-demand-generation/weight-loss-app-noom-generates-thousands-of-consumer-complaints-to-bbb))

**Lifestead must:** the settlement's terms are Lifestead's floor from the first release: renewal accepted by a box ticked on purpose, and a cancel button.

## Headway (book summaries, daily learning)

**Dislikes**
- Auto-renewal charges right after the trial, a cancellation people call intentionally confusing, refunds refused, and aggressive upgrade prompts. **many** ([Trustpilot](https://www.trustpilot.com/review/headway.co?page=4), read 2026-10-09)

**Lifestead must:** Learn (item 37), when built, is held to the across-the-board line above.

## Google Keep (leader: quick notes and lists)

**Dislikes**
- Crashes and data loss after updates. **many** ([marlvel](https://marlvel.ai/apps/com-google-keep/reviews), read 2026-10-09)
- No locking a note, little formatting, no undo, no linking one note to another, no sharing several notes at once. **many** ([Yahoo Tech](https://tech.yahoo.com/general/articles/google-keep-missing-6-basic-163023640.html); [Kimola](https://kimola.com/reports/elevate-your-app-with-google-keep-user-feedback-insights-google-play-154559))

**Lifestead must:** a capture can be locked (the vault), undone after a change, linked to a record, and shared as a group.

## Apple Notes

**Dislikes**
- After accepting new iCloud terms, Notes showed zero notes; some never came back, others only partly. **many** ([heise, 2024](https://heise.de/-10009571); [Michael Tsai, 2024-11-08](https://mjtsai.com/blog/2024/11/08/lost-icloud-notes))
- No usable export: Apple's data export loses formatting and perhaps attachments. **several** (same)

**Lifestead must:** the local database is the record, and a cloud prompt never stands between a person and it; export carries attachments.

## Drafts (quick capture)

**Dislikes**
- Drafts 5 moved custom actions behind a subscription ($19.99 a year), worked out by one user as a 1,200% rise over buying each version. **many** ([Drafts forum](https://forums.getdrafts.com/t/a-non-subscription-option-for-purchasing-drafts/7505); [Outliner Software](https://outlinersoftware.com/topics/viewt/8184))

**Wishes**
- Change: offer a way to pay once. **many** (same)

**Lifestead must:** offer a one-time or lifetime choice on the free/paid board beside the subscription, and never move something people already used into a paid tier.
