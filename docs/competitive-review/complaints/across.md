# What users of the whole-app competitors complain about

Researched 2026-10-09. Same format as `food.md`: dislikes, wishes (fix, change, add) and useless, each with source, date (the date read where the source gives none) and recurrence, ending with what Lifestead must do so the same complaint cannot be made of it.

Recurrence key: **many** (raised across many reviews or posters, or measured as a share of reviews), **several** (two to five people), **one** (a single person, kept because it is specific and checkable).

The same caution as `food.md` applies: justuseapp, Kimola, Unstar, appfollow, marlvel and appshunter are review-analysis or SEO sites, some of them competitors. Their percentages are reported as they claim them, not verified. vaultaire.app sells a photo vault of its own and codeculture.store sells to developers, so read them the same way.

"Across" holds the apps compared with something that runs through every tab rather than one of them: sync, the vault, App Lock, the hand switch, weather, and the price itself. "Most health apps" is a composite with no review channel; its complaints are the across-the-board lines of every other file in this folder. Obsidian and Obsidian Sync share one section.

---

## Across the whole category first

1. **The record lives on a company's server, and the server decides** (Keepsafe photos unreachable during outages, some never back; Gmail cut off at a storage cap). **many**
2. **Prices rise once people cannot leave** (Obsidian Sync $8 to $10 with a new vault cap, Google One rises of 19% to 300% in some countries, Google Health Premium $79.99 to $99.99, Forest's buyers moved to a subscription). **many**
3. **Billing the daily-living audience is likely to miss** (Inflow: around $200 a year after a forgotten trial, no renewal reminder, no receipt). **many**
4. **Sync that conflicts or stalls on the phone** (Obsidian on mobile). **many**
5. **A machine that answers health questions too surely** (ChatGPT: a lawsuit over advice that called a symptom not dangerous hours before a pulmonary embolism). **many**

**What Lifestead must do across the board:** the record is the database on the person's own device, so no outage and no cap can take it away (the local-first stance); a price, once paid, is never raised on the same thing, and nothing already used moves behind payment; a reminder two days before any charge, a receipt for every one, and cancelling inside the app; sync says what it is doing and what clashed (the sync activity log), on the phone as much as the computer; nothing the app says about the person diagnoses or calls a symptom nothing to worry about (`audit_clinical_claims.js`).

## ChatGPT (leader: general-purpose assistant, now with a health mode)

**Dislikes**
- A lawsuit filed by three nonprofits for a former pastor alleges ChatGPT diagnosed dysautonomia and, hours before he was hospitalised with a pulmonary embolism, called his groin tenderness not dangerous; it asks a court to pause consumer health AI until independently audited. **one** case, widely reported ([BankInfoSecurity](https://www.bankinfosecurity.com/lawsuit-claims-chatgpt-dished-out-dangerous-health-advice-a-32304); [dev.to](https://dev.to/breachprotocol/openai-launched-health-in-chatgpt-the-next-day-a-lawsuit-asked-a-court-to-pause-it-1j6j), read 2026-10-09)
- ECRI named misuse of AI chatbots the top health technology hazard for 2026. **many** (same)

**Lifestead must:** hold the five never-say rules in every generated sentence, and keep any assistant to finding and explaining the person's own records with their tier, never judging a symptom.

## Obsidian (also: Obsidian Sync; leader: local-first notes)

**Dislikes**
- Sync went from $8 to $10 a month and gained a vault-size cap it had not had; several hundred comments in 48 hours. **many** ([remio](https://www.remio.ai/post/obsidian-s-sync-pricing-hike-triggers-community-backlash), read 2026-10-09)
- Sync conflicts are the main lasting mobile complaint. **many** ([codeculture](https://codeculture.store/blogs/developer-culture/obsidian-mobile-problems-2025), read 2026-10-09)
- On iPhone a sync can take 30 minutes after many changes on the computer and stops when the screen locks; the editor freezes for minutes after it says it is done. **several** ([Obsidian forum](https://forum.obsidian.md/t/bug-ios-sync-is-very-slow-sync-should-run-in-backround/25346))
- Mobile rated "a 4" next to desktop's "10", unchanged since 2022. **many** (codeculture)

**Liked:** the notes are plain files the person owns. **many** (same)

**Lifestead must:** sync sends only what changed (delta saves), the phone is never frozen while a merge runs, and an open export is always there so the person owns the record the way Obsidian's users own their files.

## Forest (leader: focus timer)

4.4 from about 855,000 reviews.

**Dislikes**
- Buyers of the paid app moved to a subscription; heavy ads and nag screens; a wish for one-time payment. **many** ([marlvel](https://marlvel.ai/apps/forest-focus-for-productivity), read 2026-10-09; [Kimola](https://kimola.com/reports/exclusive-forest-app-user-feedback-analysis-report-google-play-en-us-151945))
- Clearing app data deletes every coin and forest. **several** (same)

**Liked:** the tree-planting idea makes focus feel rewarding. **many** (same)

**Lifestead must:** a focus or routine timer keeps its history in the encrypted record and the export, and nothing it shows is a reward that can be taken away (item 29).

## Inflow (leader: ADHD programme)

**Dislikes**
- Around $200 a year charged after a forgotten free trial, with no renewal reminder, no receipt or invoice, and cancelling hard from inside the app. **many** ([Trustpilot](https://uk.trustpilot.com/review/getinflow.io?page=5), read 2026-10-09)
- Refund emails unanswered for one to two weeks despite a "no questions asked" guarantee. **several** ([Trustpilot](https://au.trustpilot.com/review/getinflow.io?page=3))

**Liked:** some refunds given at once, with Inflow saying people with ADHD forget to cancel. **several** (same)

**Lifestead must:** the Home file's billing line, said again because Inflow sells to exactly this audience.

## Keepsafe (leader: photo vault)

justuseapp reads 66.7% of reviews as negative.

**Dislikes**
- When its servers went down people could not open their vaults, and some photos never came back. **many** ([vaultaire](https://vaultaire.app/compare/vaultaire-vs-keepsafe/review/); [justuseapp](https://justuseapp.com/en/app/510873505/secret-photo-vault-keepsafe/reviews), read 2026-10-09)
- $9.99 a month; full-screen ads on every album in the free version, with ad trackers inside a privacy app. **many** (vaultaire)

**Lifestead must:** the vault opens with no signal and no server, carries no ads or trackers on any tier, and its records travel in the encrypted copy like everything else.

## Standard Notes (encrypted notes, now part of Proton)

**Dislikes**
- After Proton bought it in 2024, users worried prices would rise and that privacy should not be "only accessible to those who can afford it"; Proton said prices were unchanged and long subscriptions honoured. **several** ([It's FOSS](https://itsfoss.com/news/proton-standard-notes/); [Privacy Guides](https://discuss.privacyguides.net/t/proton-and-standard-notes-are-joining-forces/17797), read 2026-10-09)

**Lifestead must:** App Lock and the vault belong on Free beside safety (a placement still to confirm on the free/paid board), so privacy is never what a person pays for.

## Google Health Premium (formerly Fitbit Premium)

**Dislikes**
- The rename on 2026-05-07 raised the price from $79.99 to $99.99 a year; the Gemini coach reads fitness, sleep, food, cycle data and, in the US, medical records. **many** ([Android Authority](https://androidauthority.com/google-health-premium-price-inclusions-features-3664507/); [sensai](https://www.sensai.fit/blog/google-health-premium-fitbit-ai-coach-2026), read 2026-10-09)

No dated user complaints found yet beyond the price; left for a later pass once the coach has been out longer.

**Lifestead must:** everything the coach reads, Lifestead reads on the device, with no company server and no medical records sent anywhere.

## Google One (leader: cloud storage)

**Dislikes**
- A Gmail user since 2006 stopped receiving email on hitting the 15 GB cap. **one** ([Bloomberg, 2019-10-22](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2019-10-22/gmail-hooked-us-on-free-storage-now-google-is-making-us-pay))
- Price rises of 19% in Kenya, 300% in Turkey and about 33% in the Philippines, with nothing added and no clear word on what happens to files if unpaid. **many** ([Business Daily](https://www.businessdailyafrica.com/bd/corporate/technology/google-storage-charges-up-19pc-for-kenyan-users-4481668); [sikayetvar](https://www.sikayetvar.com/en/google-one-us/i-refuse-to-pay-google-ones-500-price-hike-and-demand-a-fair-storage-option), read 2026-10-09)

**Lifestead must:** a backup that cannot be written because the person's drive is full says so plainly and keeps everything on the device; the app never needs a storage plan to keep working.

## Apple Weather (leader: built-in weather)

**Dislikes**
- Rain forecast far more often than it falls; hourly thunderstorms shown under clear skies. **many** ([Apple Discussions](https://discussions.apple.com/thread/254922784), read 2026-10-09)
- Outages and wrong data three times in one month. **many** ([gHacks](https://www.ghacks.net/?p=194296))
- Worse outside North America; less than Dark Sky, which Apple bought and closed. **many** ([Unstar](https://unstar.app/blog/weather-apps-ranked-by-user-complaints-2026))

**Lifestead must:** Garden weighs rain the person's own gauge or station measured above any forecast (Growing Conditions), and says which one a reminder used.

## One UI one-handed mode (Samsung)

**Dislikes**
- One UI 6 moved the quick toggles up out of thumb reach, against its own one-handed design. **many** ([SamMobile](https://www.sammobile.com/news/everything-about-one-handed-mode-samsung-one-ui-6-0/); [Samsung Community](https://eu.community.samsung.com/t5/galaxy-s24-series/oneui-is-not-one-handed/m-p/9487889/highlight/true), read 2026-10-09)
- A one-handed Good Lock feature removed for lack of use. **several** ([Samsung Community](https://us.community.samsung.com/t5/Discussions/One-UI-One-Handed/m-p/2641527/highlight/true))

**Lifestead must:** keep everything the navigation needs inside the thumb's reach for either hand (the hand switch), and never remove a reach feature without saying so.
