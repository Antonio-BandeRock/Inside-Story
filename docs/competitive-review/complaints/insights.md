# What users of the Insights competitors complain about

Researched 2026-10-09. Same format as `food.md`: dislikes, wishes (fix, change, add) and useless, each with source, date (the date read where the source gives none) and recurrence, ending with what Lifestead must do so the same complaint cannot be made of it.

Recurrence key: **many** (raised across many reviews or posters, or measured as a share of reviews), **several** (two to five people), **one** (a single person, kept because it is specific and checkable).

The same caution as `food.md` applies: justuseapp, Kimola, Unstar, appfollow, marlvel and appshunter are review-analysis or SEO sites, some of them competitors. Their percentages are reported as they claim them, not verified. pocketclear, checkthat.ai and costbench sell or compare against the apps they write about, so read them the same way.

Already covered elsewhere: Cronometer (Oracle and Gold are parts of it) in `food.md`. "Phone Medical ID" and "Apple Health sharing" are built into the phone rather than apps with their own reviews; what Lifestead must do about them is under the emergency card and Reports work, not here. nutritionvalue.org is a website with no review channel. SnapLabs had not been released when this was read (listed as coming 2026-02-16, with no reviews on the store), so it has no section; check again on the next pass.

---

## Across the whole category first

1. **The price climbs after people are committed.** YNAB twice in three years, Sortly from $49 to $499 a month on one plan, Drugs.com from ads to a subscription, Monarch with no free tier at all. **many**
2. **Bank and account connections break and have to be redone**, which is the whole point of a budgeting app failing (YNAB, Monarch, Mint before it closed). **many**
3. **An app that held years of somebody's records closed and took the core of it away.** Mint closed on 2024-03-23 and its replacement had no budgets at all. **many**
4. **Free trials that are too small to judge by** (KitchenPal's 50 items) or that bill after cancelling (HealthMatters.io). **several**
5. **Ads where a person is reading about medicine** (Medscape's top complaint, Drugs.com without Premium). **many**

**What Lifestead must do across the board:** a price is never raised on somebody mid-subscription without a plain, early notice and a way to keep what they have; nothing depends on a bank connection that can silently drop (records entered by the person, or imported from a file, stay theirs and stay correct); every record leaves the app in an open format at any time, so no closure can take years of it away (the life's Inside Story rule already requires this); Free is useful enough to judge the app by; no advertising, ever.

## dminder (also: D Minder Pro; leader: vitamin D from sun and supplements)

4.0 from 714 ratings; justuseapp reads its reviews as 59.5% negative.

**Dislikes**
- The developers were "rude and dismissive" when shown bugs. **one** ([justuseapp](https://justuseapp.com/en/app/547102495/d-minder-pro/reviews), read 2026-10-09)
- Bugs in the core estimate are the main thread of the negative reviews. **many** (same)

**Liked** (kept so it is not lost): adding supplements beside sun, and the sun position display. **several** (same)

**Lifestead must:** count vitamin D from food, supplement and sun as three named sources (second gap review item, sunlight as a third source), say which one supplied what, and answer every report of a wrong figure with what it was counted from.

## Sun Day (also: SunDay; vitamin D and UV tracker)

3.9; justuseapp reads 66.6% of 33 reviews as negative.

**Dislikes**
- The phone has to be aimed at the sun, when people "just want a ballpark based off the UV index". **several** ([justuseapp](https://justuseapp.com/en/app/1133953409/sunday-vitamin-d-uv-tracker/reviews), read 2026-10-09)
- Switching profiles wiped the data. **one** (same)
- A blank screen about half the time. **one** (same)

**Wishes**
- Add: more skin tones to choose from. **several** (same)

**Lifestead must:** estimate sun from time outdoors and the day's UV index with no aiming, offer the full Fitzpatrick range of skin types, and never lose a record when anything about the profile changes.

## Drugs.com (leader: drug reference and interaction checker; also Drugs.com Pill Identifier)

4.8 from 34,908 ratings.

**Dislikes**
- Moving from ads to an $8.99 a month subscription, read as "greedy". **several** ([marlvel](https://marlvel.ai/apps/drugs-com-medication-guide), read 2026-10-09)
- Version 3.1.51 (March 2026) brought login failures. **several** (same)
- The ads are annoying without Premium. **many** (same)
- The pill identifier returned wrong, too many or no results for common OTC pills (acetaminophen, Excedrin) in Consumer Reports testing, though Drugs.com was among the most accurate tested. **one** test ([Consumer Reports, 2014-04](https://www.consumerreports.org/cro/news/2014/04/best-pill-identifier-apps/index.htm))

**Liked:** the interaction checker. **many** (marlvel)

**Lifestead must:** keep interaction warnings Free and ad-free (P28 already says safety is never behind a paywall); never require an account sign-in to read a warning; anything that names a pill says how sure it is and that a pharmacist confirms it.

## Medscape (leader: clinical reference)

justuseapp reads 66.7% of 1,384 reviews as negative.

**Dislikes**
- Advertising is the biggest complaint. **many** ([justuseapp](https://justuseapp.com/en/app/321367289/medscape/reviews), read 2026-10-09)
- Tapping the search bar crashed the app. **several** (same)
- The weight-based dose calculator returned wrong doses. **one** (same)
- The pill identifier was removed. **several** ([marlvel](https://marlvel.ai/apps/medscape), read 2026-10-09)

**Lifestead must:** carry no ads; never calculate a dose (the clinical-claims rule already forbids it); never remove a function people rely on without saying so in the app and pointing to where it went.

## HealthMatters.io (leader: lab result history and explanation)

TrustScore 3 from 7 reviews.

**Dislikes**
- Signed up for a trial, found it did not support the person's bloodwork, cancelled at once and was billed monthly anyway, with trouble getting it resolved. **one** ([Trustpilot](https://www.trustpilot.com/review/healthmatters.io), read 2026-10-09)

**Liked:** each test explained with a grade, export to Excel, and trends across years. **several** (same)

**Lifestead must:** take any lab value, with its unit and the lab's own range, so no one's bloodwork is unsupported; export labs to an open spreadsheet format; cancelling is one step inside the app.

## BloodTrends (bloodwork tracker)

**Dislikes:** none dated found. The reviews read are positive: easy biomarker entry, clear LOW and HIGH tags, trends that help the doctor conversation ([appshunter](https://appshunter.io/ios/app/bloodtrends-bloodwork-tracker/id6748859800), read 2026-10-09). Parsing a lab PDF is Pro only.

**Lifestead must:** match its plainness (enter a value, see where it sits against the lab's range and the person's usual range) and decide on the free/paid board whether reading a lab PDF is Free, since that is BloodTrends' paid line.

## Carrot Care (bloodwork tracker)

4.3 from 146 ratings ([App Store](https://apps.apple.com/app/id1518603405), read 2026-10-09). No dated complaints found; the praise is for the many ways to enter a result ([alternativeto](https://alternativeto.net/software/carrot-care)). Left for a later pass.

**Lifestead must:** take a lab result typed, photographed or from a file.

## Spokin (leader: food allergy community and products)

4.6 from 886 reviews.

**Dislikes**
- "Completely unusable": search will not take Enter and ignores location even when location is allowed. **several** ([justuseapp](https://justuseapp.com/en/app/1201909035/spokin-manage-food-allergies/reviews), read 2026-10-09)
- A restaurant can be found but its menu cannot be seen. **one** (same)

**Liked:** finding safe products and reading what other allergic people say about them. **several** (same)

**Lifestead must:** make search finish on Enter everywhere; allergy cautions say "allergen-aware, not allergy-safe" (open item 26); a restaurant or product saved with what the person checked about it.

## YNAB (leader: budgeting)

$14.99 a month or $109 a year.

**Dislikes**
- Price rises in 2022 and 2024; the rise from $99 to $109 was announced by email after people had renewed, and legacy users went from $60 to nearly double. **many** ([pocketclear price timeline](https://pocketclear.app/blog/ynab-price-increases-history.html), read 2026-10-09)
- Bank sync drops, comes in late or duplicates transactions, and asks for credentials again. **many** ([pocketclear](https://pocketclear.app/blog/ynab-complaints-problems.html), read 2026-10-09)
- r/ynab now carries regular complaint threads. **many** ([Unstar](https://unstar.app/blog/ynab-rocket-money-monarch-everydollar-copilot-budget-apps-ranked-2026), read 2026-10-09)

**Lifestead must:** Life > Finances needs no bank connection to be useful, imports a bank's CSV export without duplicating a transaction already entered, and any price change follows the rule at the top of this file.

## Monarch (also: Monarch Money; budgeting)

$99.99 a year or $14.99 a month, no free tier after a 7-day trial.

**Dislikes**
- Account connections break repeatedly (Mr. Cooper, Rocket Mortgage, RBC, Capital One, Fidelity, Schwab, Wealthsimple). **many** ([checkthat.ai](https://checkthat.ai/brands/monarch/reviews), read 2026-10-09)
- No free tier. **several** (same)

**Lifestead must:** the same as YNAB; Finances stays usable on Free in the shape the free/paid board decides.

## Mint (closed 2024-03-23)

**Dislikes**
- Intuit closed it and moved users to Credit Karma, which kept transactions and net worth but dropped budgets with limits, savings goals and spending trends. **many** ([NerdWallet](https://www.nerdwallet.com/article/finance/mint-app-closing-what-it-means-how-to-pick-a-new-budget-service), [CNBC, 2023-11-07](https://www.cnbc.com/2023/11/07/budgeting-app-mint-is-shutting-down-users-are-disappointed.html))
- A "full-stop hard migration" rather than linking. **several** ([Finary](https://finary.com/en/blog/mint-is-shutting-down-what-you-need-to-know/))
- About 3.6 million active users found no free app doing what it did. **many** (CNBC)

**Lifestead must:** keep budgets, goals and trends in Finances; every record exports in an open format so nothing depends on Lifestead staying open.

## Sortly (leader: home inventory)

**Dislikes**
- One plan went from $49 to $499 a month. **several** ([costbench](https://www.costbench.com/software/inventory-management/sortly/hidden-costs/), read 2026-10-09)
- Somebody paid over $299 a month to inventory a home. **one** ([Trustpilot](https://pl.trustpilot.com/review/sortly.com?page=2), read 2026-10-09)

**Lifestead must:** home inventory (Kitchen, Where did I put it, Grow Setup) is part of the person's plan, never priced per item or per seat in the way a business tool is.

## KitchenPal (pantry and recipes)

justuseapp reads 65.4% of reviews as positive.

**Dislikes**
- Stops at 50 items before paying: "I won't buy it if I can't try it". **several** ([justuseapp](https://justuseapp.com/en/app/1084982489/kitchenpal-pantry-recipes/reviews), read 2026-10-09)
- Closes on its own. **several** (same)
- A husband could not share the kitchen. **one** (same)

**Lifestead must:** no item cap on the Kitchen; two people share it through the household tier, with the shopping list already merging between people.

## Pantry Check (pantry and grocery list)

4.5 from 162 ratings ([App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/pantry-check-grocery-list/id966702368)).

**Wishes**
- Add: a restock level, so an item running low goes onto the shopping list by itself. **several** ([justuseapp](https://justuseapp.com/en/app/966702368/pantry-check-grocery-list/reviews), read 2026-10-09)

**Lifestead must:** a kitchen item carries an optional "put it on the list when I have this many left", and the list says why each such item is there.
